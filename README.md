I am an agricultural researcher specializing in **sustainable crop protection**, **plant disease epidemiology**, and **molecular diagnostics**. My work bridges hand-on agronomic experimentation with advanced data science pipelines in **R** and **Python** to enhance crop productivity and optimize plant health management, with a focused application in citrus research.

* 🔬 **Research Interests:** Plant pathology, crop protection, molecular diagnostics, and epidemiology.
* 📊 **Analytics & Modeling:** Linear mixed-effects models (REML/BLUP), multi-trait genotype selection (MGIDI), PCA, and multivariate clustering.
* 🌐 **Open Science Advocate:** Developing reproducible analytical workflows and sharing open-source R code for agricultural science.
