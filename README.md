## 🌿 About Me

I am a researcher focused on **crop protection**, **plant health**, and **sustainable agriculture**, specializing in molecular diagnostics and disease management. My research integrates hands-on field and greenhouse experimentation with statistical modeling and computational pipelines in **R** and **Python** to solve complex agricultural challenges, particularly in citrus research.

- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Plant pathology, crop protection, molecular diagnostics, and disease epidemiology.
- 📊 **Data Science & Statistics:** Advanced mixed-effects models (REML/BLUP), PCA, clustering, and multi-trait selection indices (MGIDI).
- 🤝 **Community & Mentorship:** Open science advocate sharing analytical frameworks and R workflows with agricultural students and researchers.
